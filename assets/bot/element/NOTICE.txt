model restriction occurs in the pinned Element subtree. Model and code were
added by lucas-emery in commit 0e2f63dce48e72a1c28f9ace6361e8386b60c753.

Adaptation: ONNX export of the unmodified trained actor, flattened probability
heads, TypeScript observations/controller decoding, and Element's own dynamic
speedflip kickoff. No Nexto/Necto code, models or kickoff table are used.
Observations use native car orientation, wheel contact and flip availability;
these replace the original RLBot wrapper's Euler reconstruction, six-tick
contact grace and approximate double-jump flag. The previous raw policy action
is preserved even when the controller suppresses yaw during a jump.
Kickoff angle dot products are clamped for numerical stability.
This is a historical 1v1 model; difficulty labels do not certify a human rank.
